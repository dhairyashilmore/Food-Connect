# 🍽️ Food Connect – Food Donation Platform

## 📌 Project Overview
**Food Connect** is a social impact web application developed to help people who do not have access to even one proper meal a day. The platform simplifies the food donation process by connecting food donors with people in need, helping reduce food waste and hunger through technology.

The website allows donors to share excess food by providing details such as the quantity of food and pickup location. This food is then collected and distributed to needy individuals, ensuring that safe and edible food reaches the right people at the right time.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Reduce food wastage
- Help needy people get timely meals
- Create a simple and accessible food donation platform
- Build a bridge between food donors and recipients

---

## ⚙️ How It Works
1. Donor visits the website
2. Donor enters food details (quantity, type, pickup location)
3. System records donation information
4. Food is collected from the donor
5. Food is delivered to people in need
